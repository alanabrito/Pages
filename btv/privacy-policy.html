btv Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

btv is an iPhone app that helps users browse subscribed YouTube channels, play videos, and optionally control playback with Meta glasses integrations. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how that data is used, and what third-party services are involved.

Information We Access

Depending on how you use the app, btv may access:

  • Your Google account sign-in session for YouTube access.
  • Your YouTube subscriptions, liked videos, and channel/video metadata available through the YouTube API.
  • Playback state and locally stored preferences, such as watched videos, playback position, and accessibility or UI settings.
  • Meta glasses registration state and app-link actions if you connect Meta wearables.

How We Use Information

The app uses this information only to provide core functionality, including:

  • Signing you in with Google.
  • Loading your YouTube subscriptions and videos.
  • Tracking watched state and playback position on your device.
  • Supporting narration, accessibility, and playback controls.
  • Connecting and responding to Meta glasses actions when enabled by you.

Data Storage

btv stores some information locally on your device, including authentication tokens, watched video state, last-viewed channels, playback positions, and app preferences. This data is used only to make the app function and improve continuity between sessions.

Data Sharing

btv does not sell your personal data. The app relies on third-party services to function, and data may be exchanged directly with those services as needed for the features you use.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In for account authentication.
  • YouTube Data API for subscriptions, channels, and video metadata.
  • Meta Wearables / Meta AI integration if you choose to connect Meta glasses.

Analytics and Tracking

btv does not include its own advertising SDK or sell user profiles for advertising. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before release.

Your Choices

  • You can sign out of Google from within the app.
  • You can disconnect Meta glasses from within the app settings.
  • You can remove the app from your device to delete locally stored app data.

Children’s Privacy

btv is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions about btv, please contact the app publisher through the project repository: github.com/alanabrito/youtube-embed.
