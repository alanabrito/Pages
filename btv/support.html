btv Support

Last updated: March 13, 2026

btv helps you browse your YouTube subscriptions, play videos with accessible controls, and optionally connect Meta glasses for hands-free actions.

Contact

For support, bug reports, or account questions, please use the project repository: github.com/alanabrito/youtube-embed/issues.

What to Include in a Support Request

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version you are using.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • Whether VoiceOver, Google sign-in, YouTube playback, or Meta glasses were involved.
  • Any error message shown in the app.

Troubleshooting

  • If Google sign-in fails, confirm the app build is using the correct production OAuth client ID.
  • If videos do not load, check your internet connection and make sure the YouTube API key is configured correctly for the build.
  • If Meta glasses commands do not work, reconnect the app from Meta AI and verify the app is registered.
  • If narration or VoiceOver behavior seems incorrect, fully close and reopen the app, then try the same transition again.
