Concept + focusbtv is an iPhone app that turns YouTube into a channel-surfing experience built around VoiceOver: browse subscribed channels, get videos narrated before they play, and skip forward, back, or to the next channel without hunting for small on-screen controls.
VoiceOver-firstYouTubenarrated playback
Core promiseSign in once, then everything after that is built for ears and gestures — not eyes and precise taps.

Key features

  • Browse YouTube subscriptions and continue where you left off.
  • Spoken video details (title, channel, duration, publish date) before each video plays.
  • Play, pause, next, and previous from the lock screen or any connected Bluetooth accessory.
  • Fully VoiceOver-annotated controls throughout, with swipe and hardware-key navigation.

Audience

Designed for blind and low-vision users who want to watch YouTube content from channels they already follow, without depending on a sighted layout to find the next video.
