BlindMessaging

Messaging that prioritizes flow over friction.

BlindMessaging is a communication concept built around audio-first interaction, fast message review, and low-vision friendly controls that reduce the amount of interface management required.

SupportPrivacy
LicensePublishing docs
Brand directionConfident, direct, and conversational. The product voice should feel efficient without losing warmth.
Core promiseHelp users send, hear, and manage messages quickly with less screen friction.

Key features

  • Voice-first compose and review.
  • Fast playback of recent conversations.
  • Large targets and reduced gesture complexity.
  • Optional text and audio message modes.

Audience

BlindMessaging is designed for users who want messaging workflows optimized for speech, listening, speed, and clarity rather than visually dense conversation interfaces.
