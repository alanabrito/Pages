BlindMessaging

Messaging with less friction.

A communication concept built around audio-first interaction, fast message review, and low-vision friendly controls that cut down how much interface you have to manage.

Support Privacy License

The concept

Messaging interfaces are visually dense by default — threads, timestamps, reactions, nested replies. This strips it back to listening, speaking, and moving on.

Audio-first Speed Clarity Speech
01

Core promise

Send, hear, and manage messages quickly, with far less screen friction between you and the conversation.

02

Who it's for

Users who want messaging workflows optimised for speech, listening, and speed rather than dense visual conversation views.

03

Status

Product concept in active design.

Key features

What it does.

Features

  • Voice-first compose and review.
  • Fast playback of recent conversations.
  • Large targets and reduced gesture complexity.
  • Optional text and audio message modes.

Design principle

Speed is accessibility.

For a screen-reader user, every extra step compounds. Cutting a three-gesture action to one is not a convenience — it's the difference between a tool people use and one they work around.
