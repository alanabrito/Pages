Privacy policy

BlindMessaging accesses only what it needs to work.

BlindMessaging is currently a product concept. This page describes the privacy approach the app is designed around: collect the minimum, keep it on the device, and never sell it.

Information accessed

  • Only the data required for the app to function, described in the app at the point of request.
  • Locally stored preferences and accessibility settings.

How it is used

  • Providing the app's core functionality.
  • Remembering your preferences on your device.

Storage and sharing

  • Preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • No personal data is sold, and the app contains no advertising SDK.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Questions: alanabas@gmail.com.
