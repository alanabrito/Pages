Privacy Policy

BlindMessaging uses communication and accessibility data to power messaging features.

Depending on implementation, BlindMessaging may access contacts, microphone input, notifications, and message metadata to enable sending, reviewing, and receiving messages. Any collection should be limited to core functionality and disclosed clearly before release.

Summary

  • Contacts and message metadata for communication flow.
  • Microphone access for voice compose.
  • No sale of personal data.
