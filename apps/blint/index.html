blint

Watch your subscriptions, hands-free.

An iPhone app that turns YouTube into a channel-surfing experience built around VoiceOver — browse subscribed channels, hear what a video is before it plays, and move between them without hunting for small on-screen controls.

Support Privacy License

The concept

Sign in once. After that, everything is built for ears and gestures rather than eyes and precise taps.

VoiceOver-first YouTube Data API Narrated playback iOS
01

Core promise

Every control is labelled, every transition is narrated, and playback is fully drivable from a connected Bluetooth accessory, a headphone remote, or a keyboard.

02

Who it's for

Blind and low-vision users who want to watch content from channels they already follow, without depending on a sighted layout to find the next video.

03

Status

Built and in pre-launch — working through App Store submission.

Key features

What it does.

Features

  • Browse YouTube subscriptions and continue where you left off.
  • Spoken video details — title, channel, duration, publish date — before each video plays.
  • Play, pause, next, and previous from a Bluetooth accessory, headphone remote, or keyboard.
  • Fully VoiceOver-annotated controls, with swipe and hardware-key navigation throughout.

Design principle

Narration is the interface.

Most accessible apps add labels to a visual layout. blint inverts it: the audio sequence is the primary design artifact, and the screen is what happens to be showing while you listen.
