Concept + focusblint is a concept for quick audio-first orientation. It turns nearby context into short, useful cues so movement feels more confident, less noisy, and more immediate.
audio-firstorientationconfidence
Core promiseDeliver what matters now: where you are, what changed, and what to do next.

Key features

  • Rapid audio cues with concise phrasing.
  • Nearby landmark and direction awareness.
  • Simple haptic confirmations for key actions.
  • Low-friction interface for repeat use while moving.

Audience

Designed for blind and low-vision users who want fast spatial orientation without a heavy navigation workflow every time they move through a familiar or semi-familiar place.
