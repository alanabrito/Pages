Privacy Policy

blint only accesses what it needs to sign you in and play your subscriptions.

blint is an iPhone app that helps users browse subscribed YouTube channels and play videos with accessible, narrated controls. This page explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and what third-party services are involved. Last updated: August 16, 2026.

Summary

  • Google sign-in session for YouTube access.
  • YouTube subscriptions, liked videos, and video metadata.
  • Locally stored playback state and preferences.
  • No selling of personal data, no ad SDK.

Information we access

  • Your Google account sign-in session for YouTube access.
  • Your YouTube subscriptions, liked videos, and channel/video metadata available through the YouTube API.
  • Playback state and locally stored preferences, such as watched videos, playback position, and accessibility or UI settings.

How we use it

  • Signing you in with Google.
  • Loading your YouTube subscriptions and videos.
  • Tracking watched state and playback position on your device.
  • Supporting narration, accessibility, and playback controls.

Data storage

blint stores some information locally on your device, including authentication tokens, watched video state, last-viewed channels, playback positions, and app preferences. This data is used only to make the app function and improve continuity between sessions.

Data sharing

blint does not sell your personal data. The app relies on third-party services to function, and data may be exchanged directly with those services as needed for the features you use.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In for account authentication.
  • YouTube Data API for subscriptions, channels, and video metadata.
  • The embedded YouTube player, which loads video content directly from YouTube. YouTube may set its own cookies and collect viewing data under Google's Privacy Policy, independently of blint.

Analytics and tracking

blint does not include its own advertising SDK, does not run its own analytics, and does not sell user profiles for advertising. Note that video playback happens in YouTube's own embedded player, so YouTube's tracking applies to what you watch. If blint's own practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated before release.

Your choices

  • Signing out from within the app also revokes blint's access to your Google account, so it no longer appears under your Google account's "Third-party apps with account access".
  • You can review or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can remove the app from your device to delete locally stored app data.

Children's privacy

blint is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates are posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact
