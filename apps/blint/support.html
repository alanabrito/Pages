blint Support

Setup, features, and troubleshooting.

blint helps you browse your YouTube subscriptions and play videos with accessible, narrated controls. Everything below covers getting started and fixing the most common issues.

Contact

  • Email alanabas@gmail.com for support, bug reports, or account questions.
  • Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and app version.
  • Describe the problem and whether VoiceOver was involved.

What to include in a support request

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version you are using.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • Whether VoiceOver, Google sign-in, or YouTube playback were involved.
  • Any error message shown in the app.

Troubleshooting

  • If Google sign-in fails, confirm the app build is using the correct production OAuth client ID.
  • If videos do not load, check your internet connection and make sure the YouTube API key is configured correctly for the build.
  • If narration or VoiceOver behavior seems incorrect, fully close and reopen the app, then try the same transition again.
