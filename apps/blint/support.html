Support

Setup, features, and troubleshooting.

Everything below covers getting started with blint and fixing the most common issues.

Contact

  • Email alanabas@gmail.com for support, bug reports, or account questions.
  • Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and app version.
  • Describe the problem and whether VoiceOver was involved.

What to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version you are using.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • Whether VoiceOver, sign-in, or playback were involved.
  • Any error message shown in the app.

Troubleshooting

  • If Google sign-in fails, confirm the app build is using the correct production OAuth client ID.
  • If videos do not load, check your internet connection and confirm the YouTube API key is configured for the build.
  • If narration or VoiceOver behaviour seems incorrect, fully close and reopen the app, then repeat the same transition.

Response time

This is an independent project maintained outside of working hours. Expect a reply within a few days — accessibility-blocking bugs get priority.
