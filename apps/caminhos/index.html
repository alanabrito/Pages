Caminhos

Navigation with calm.

Concept + focusCaminhos is a route companion for blind and low-vision users, designed around trust, pacing, and environmental awareness. It aims to make guidance feel supportive instead of overwhelming.
trustpacingcontext
Core promiseRoute guidance that respects pace, reduces overload, and keeps important context close at hand.

Key features

  • Accessible route previews with concise summaries.
  • Step-by-step spoken guidance.
  • Hazard and transition awareness.
  • Quiet mode for low-frequency prompts.

Audience

Caminhos is for people who want stronger orientation during planned travel, with a softer and more confidence-building tone than conventional navigation products.
