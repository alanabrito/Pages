Caminhos

Navigation with a calmer voice.

A route companion for blind and low-vision travellers, designed around trust, pacing, and environmental awareness — guidance that feels supportive instead of relentless.

Support Privacy License

The concept

Conventional navigation optimises for information density. Caminhos optimises for confidence: fewer prompts, better timed, with the context that actually matters at a junction.

Core promise

Route guidance that respects your pace, reduces overload, and keeps important context close at hand.

02

Who it's for

People who want stronger orientation during planned travel, with a tone that builds confidence rather than anxiety.

03

Status

Product concept in active design.

Key features

What it does.

Features

  • Accessible route previews with concise, scannable summaries.
  • Step-by-step spoken guidance timed to walking pace.
  • Hazard and transition awareness at the moments that matter.
  • Quiet mode for low-frequency prompting on familiar routes.

Design principle

Silence is a feature.

A navigation app that talks constantly trains you to tune it out. Caminhos spends its attention budget carefully so that when it does speak, you listen.
