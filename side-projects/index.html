03 — Why

Building for a screen reader changes what you consider finished.

When the interface is audio, you cannot hide a weak information hierarchy behind a nice layout. Every label, every order of operations, every piece of state has to be genuinely correct — because it is being read aloud, one item at a time, to someone who cannot skim ahead.

That discipline follows me into everything I spec at work — and it is the main reason I keep shipping these, even when the day job is already full.
